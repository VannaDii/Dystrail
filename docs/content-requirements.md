# Dystrail content requirements

Status: proposed production brief, September 13, 2026. The quantities below are editorial targets for repeat play. They do not change the game’s rules or represent hard session limits. The companion [unit hit list](content-hit-list.md) gives every deliverable a stable editorial ID, mode, use, and review/write status.

1. **Define a complete unit.**

   One narrative unit is a playable scene or vignette with its setup, required responses, and outcome text. An encounter with three choices is one unit with three branches. Translations, alternate names, portraits, and minor rewordings do not count as additional units. Every variant must offer a different situation, observation, or payoff. A/B/C identify separately authored variants, not a sequence that every player must see.

2. **Set the repeat-play target.**

   Validate at least three fresh playthroughs per mode and route: six routes × two modes × three runs = 36 completed journeys, plus checks for failure outcomes. Aim for at least 80% of road-scene variants in each fresh run to be unseen in the preceding two runs. Each town must offer three different conversations before repeating one, regardless of mode. Exact replay is allowed to repeat the original content. Very long runs and indefinite replay require graceful reuse; a finite writing budget cannot guarantee perpetual novelty.

4. **Road encounters: produce 231 complete scenes.**

   Expand the 65 existing encounter families to three substantive scenes apiece: 18 Classic families × 3 = 54; 13 Deep End families × 3 = 39; 34 shared families × 3 = 102. Preserve each existing family’s eligibility and its two or three ordered mechanical choices while writing new situations that make those choices plausible. This existing-family work represents 195 scenes and 561 choice/outcome pairs.

   Add 12 new western families: two Classic and two Deep End families for each of Pacific Coast, Mountain West, and Southwest. Give each three scene variants, producing 36 additional scenes. Their mechanical choices remain to be designed and reviewed; reserve three choice/outcome pairs per scene for writing capacity, or 108 pairs. The complete road budget is therefore 231 scenes and up to 669 choice/outcome pairs under that proposal.

   Shared scenes must work in both modes. Mode-exclusive scenes should reflect their actual opportunities, pressures, and consequences. A harsher joke alone does not establish a different mechanical consequence. Allocate money/work, food/supply support, evidence/information, recovery, access, and movement situations across the eligible pools. The hit list records the uses supported by current choices.

5. **Western additions: write the following 12 new families.**

   These are research and design briefs, not verified factual premises or finished runtime keys. Their three variants must establish distinct local situations.

   - Pacific Coast / Classic: freight prices and household purchasing; community access to services and infrastructure.
   - Pacific Coast / Deep End: contested grants and documentary evidence; information control and access to public records.
   - Mountain West / Classic: essential services over long distances; energy work and local mutual aid.
   - Mountain West / Deep End: research funding and institutional costs; land, energy, and permitting claims.
   - Southwest / Classic: water access and everyday work; food distribution and transport costs.
   - Southwest / Deep End: heat risk and official denial; trade promises and local livelihoods.

   Count each family once even if it can appear in several places. Adding cross-region tags does not create new content. Research the underlying claims before drafting attributed facts.

6. **Town conversations: produce 132 sourced scene packages.**

   There are 44 distinct intermediate towns across the six routes. Give each three conversations: 44 × 3 = 132. Each package needs a distinct, locally applicable factual angle, its source/date/qualification, and a short fictional resident comment that follows from it. Three jokes attached to an unchanged fact are insufficient for the target of three different conversations. Multiple angles may use one reliable source when it supports each claim.

   Retain one context record for each of the six origins and D.C. Those seven endpoints do not currently function as intermediate town-conversation stops. This creates a research target of **139 local fact/angle records: 132 conversation records plus seven endpoint records**, compared with today’s 51 baseline records. Do not present the seven endpoint records as additional conversations a player currently encounters.

   Maintain **51 town profiles** with the place name, state, population/year/source, and attraction/source. These are factual reference copy, separate from the satirical conversation packages. The hit list names all 44 conversation towns and all seven endpoints.

7. **Care: produce 24 scenarios with continuity.**

   Expand eight incident families to three variants each: raw-milk illness; heat/dehydration; administrative overwork; wellness-product sickness; navigation exhaustion; repair-cost disputes; intrusive workplace messaging; and strain from substitute food-distribution labor.

   Each variant needs an initial setup, a persistent/worsening follow-up, and a critical warning. Cover the three decisions—provide care, leave a companion in shelter, or press on—and their five actual outcomes: helped, sheltered, deferred, companion lost, or player lost. Shelter is unavailable for the player. The current game uses five shared outcome messages; individual scenario responses are a proposed writing and integration expansion.

   These are 24 scenarios with follow-up states, not 72 unrelated incidents. A continuing injury stays with the same person and reason. Recovery clears that continuity. Write serious consequences directly and humanely; aim the satire at the policy, institution, or claim behind the situation.

8. **Ally departures: produce 18 vignettes.**

   Expand six existing reasons to three versions: political defection; job loss; extra work caused by repair costs; merchandise opportunism; misinformation and withdrawal; and injury while protesting. Include a conditional final-ally response when the support network reaches zero. These contacts are separate from the six named travelers. Never imply a named crew member departed when only the allies counter changed.

9. **Crossings: produce 12 packages for four actual mode/stage combinations.**

   Write three variants for each: first checkpoint, shared; second checkpoint, Classic; second bridge obstruction, Deep End; final bridge obstruction, shared. Cover passage, diversion, permit use, bribe success/failure, and terminal failure where those branches are possible. The first checkpoint’s refused-passage outcome becomes a detour and must not claim the expedition died there.

   A run has three crossing milestones. Crossings currently resolve from game state and policy; this brief does not assume a new player-choice screen. Use actual travel outcome/journal surfaces unless a separate interface change is approved. The milestone values are internal progress units, not fixed real-world towns or distances across all routes.

10. **Political bulletins: produce 18 executive-order packages.**

    Write three variants for each game-defined order: Shutdown, Militarize, Deregulate, Tax Cuts, Tariffs, and Gag Order. Each needs activation copy, a concise explanation of its actual gameplay effect, and expiration/relief text. Distinguish these simulated events from sourced historical claims. Use current effect values through the game’s data rather than embedding invented costs or durations in jokes.

11. **Breakdowns and repairs: produce 12 packages.**

    Write three variants for each failing part: tire, battery, alternator, and fuel pump. Every package covers four repair paths: use an onboard spare; buy a replacement; barter supplies; and work through local-radio contacts. The purchase response must distinguish town service from roadside delivery. A zero-cash player must receive accurate copy for the available work option. Do not imply a part was bought, consumed, or repaired before that action succeeds.

12. **Routine activities: produce 24 short packages.**

    Write three variants for each of eight uses: forage with a guide; glean a farm; work for food; paid unloading work; barter supplies for a tire; barter supplies for a battery; barter a tire for supplies; and full rest. Each includes a clear offer/action and a matching completion line. Preserve the actual cost, reward, timing, and once-per-stop/cooldown rules. Do not commission additional therapy or hack-repair scenes solely because old translation keys still exist; those actions are currently disabled.

13. **Travel conditions: produce 27 short packages.**

    Write three variants for each of nine uses: clear weather, storm, heat wave, cold snap, smoke, ordinary illness, hunger, heat exposure, and cold exposure. Distinguish ambient weather from actual damage, illness from the separate care incidents, and risk from confirmed harm. Where relevant, include onset, continuing-condition, and recovery/mitigation lines. Use an outcome surface that already exists; do not interrupt travel with a new modal for every flavor line.

14. **Openings and the hearing: produce 24 packages.**

    Provide three departure introductions for each of six persona/origin pairs: Journalist/Seattle; Organizer/Portland; Whistleblower/San Francisco; Lobbyist/Los Angeles; Staffer/Sacramento; Satirist/San Diego. This gives 18 shared opening packages grounded in the actual character and starting place. Their display at departure is a proposed integration task.

    Provide three hearing introductions/transitions per mode, giving six packages. The current hearing applies three configured stamina rounds and then one vote roll; it does not hold three independent player votes. Explain and dramatize only the mechanics actually present. The separate ending packages own the final outcome and epilogue.

15. **Endings: cover 15 result uses with three versions each, for 45 packages.**

    Cover the 13 typed outcomes: hearing victory; failed vote; sanity loss; destroyed vehicle; cold exposure; heat exposure; and collapse caused by hunger, vehicle trouble, weather, breakdown, disease, crossing, or panic. Also cover an unfinished/abandoned journey and a generic fallback for incomplete or legacy result data. Each package includes a headline and short epilogue. Some typed causes support older or uncommon states; identify them accurately rather than assuming every cause occurs in every run.

    Endings must agree with the journey record, surviving crew, actual cause, and whether the player ever reached D.C. Never invent a completed hearing or a death to make the joke work.

16. **Maintain 77 factual and functional records outside the narrative total.**

    The supporting checklist contains seven endpoint-context records, 51 town profiles, 11 shop-item descriptions, six persona descriptions, and two mode descriptions. The 132 sourced conversation records are already included in the town packages. Keep item effects, affordability, costs, travel timing, navigation, save/replay controls, and help text precise. Humor may accompany a decision but must not obscure it. Existing accurate utility text can remain unchanged after review.

17. **Make each unit ready for a writer and an integrator.**

    Record its editorial ID; existing runtime family/key or proposed integration point; Classic/Deep End/shared scope; exact town or eligible regions; gameplay use; source hook and evidence status; setup; ordered choices; every applicable outcome; names/variables; required cast/state; variant/family relationship; illustration needs; and review status. Keep source claims and fictional speech separate. Preserve supplied names and absence/death state. Do not invent mechanical grants, consent, affiliations, or changes of location inside flavor text.

    Draft in English, then deliver English, Spanish, Italian, and Arabic with equivalent meaning and valid interpolation. A unit is accepted only after its source, joke logic, gameplay fit, localization, and rendered placement pass review. Establish the premise, make the action plausible, and let the payoff follow directly. Keep the writing witty, sharp, and short.

18. **Require selection support before claiming repeat-play variety.**

    Maintain distinct family IDs and variant IDs. Favor unseen families as well as unseen text, so a run does not encounter the same underlying scene repeatedly with fresh wording. Remember content across new runs, including shared content seen in either mode; persist the current run’s full seen set across saves. Use history-aware selection for town, care, ally, and supporting variants where selection is currently fixed or a short numeric cycle. An ongoing care incident deliberately retains its chosen variant.

    Save the chosen variants so reload and exact replay reproduce them. Resolve the compatibility between history-aware fresh runs and existing seed/share-code replay before implementation: a bare seed cannot by itself encode a player’s different prior-content history. Do not silently change the current replay promise or alter simulation randomness to add cosmetic variety.

    Regional eligibility, truthful consequences, available crew, and affordable choices take priority over novelty. When a pool is exhausted, reuse the least recently seen eligible content without blocking the game. Avoid adjacent scenes built on the same factual hook when another eligible hook is available. If the 36-run check misses the novelty target, expand the affected region/mode/use rather than declaring the overall inventory count sufficient.

19. **Produce in this order.**

    First review the existing 65 encounters, 44 playable town packages, eight care scenarios, and six ally vignettes. Then fill the western mode-specific gaps and complete second/third town conversations. Next finish road variants and care/ally continuity. Complete one accurate version of every supporting and ending branch before its additional variants. Integrate selection, run the coverage/novelty checks, and expand the specific pools that fall short.

20. **Retain the current gameplay limits as context.**

    The current encounter bank has 65 families and 187 choices: 18 Classic-only, 13 Deep End-only, and 34 shared. Each current route admits up to 50 Classic or 45 Deep End families across its regions. There are 15–21 intermediate town stops per route, 51 distinct places across all routes, eight care reasons, six ally-loss reasons, three crossing milestones, six named travelers, and one final hearing. Encounters are limited to two per game day; care checks are at least five days apart. The configured 8–30 days is a pacing target, not a hard session cap.

    This proposal supplies 162 eligible road variants per current Classic route and 147 per Deep End route, before any additional eligibility design for new western families. Those totals assume each proposed western family stays in its assigned region. They are available pools, not promised encounter counts in a playthrough. A’s existing prose must pass review before being credited; B/C require new scenes.
