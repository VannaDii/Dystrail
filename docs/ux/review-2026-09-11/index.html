    Progress worth preserving

    The foundations now read as this game.

    Protect the art direction

    Use the approved portraits and normal van as style anchors: chunky pixel contours, restrained colors, simplified volumes and readable expressions. Several encounter scenes use softer shading; bring them back to those anchors.

    Next art priorities: distinct town arrivals, actual recovery, named crew incidents and endings. Review region, time, weather, active cast and anatomy inside the real HUD crop. A correct file assignment is only the beginning of scene continuity.

    Recommended sequence

    One coherent leg before another art batch.

    01

    Repair trust

    Fix scroll entry, truthful effective gains, the latest receipt, journal persistence, clock/rest behavior and the nearly invisible persona origin.

    Exit check: the player can explain what changed, why, and when.

    02

    Restore the travel rhythm

    Let Resume carry a leg. Pause for meaningful incidents or player intervention. Put urgent consequences before a periodic map review.

    Exit check: one natural leg feels like traveling, with decisions inside it.

    03

    Make places and people matter

    Give arrivals their own scene and services. Let named crew participate, suffer, recover and leave persistent consequences. Keep every scene's cast eligible.

    Exit check: the player remembers a person and a place, not just a stat change.

    04

    Pay off the trip

    Establish the purpose of D.C. early. Connect accumulated resources and relationships to the final challenge. End with the crew's story, then detailed scoring.

    Exit check: the ending feels earned by this particular journey.

    Let the satire become a constraint you can act on.

    A fictional premium queue at a river crossing can mean cash versus detour time. A cooling center can mean donating supplies versus continued exposure. A renamed permit can make an earlier receipt useful. Show the actual cost and let a named crew member help.

    Use local industries, civic branding and institutional incentives for regional jokes. Preserve real geography, and give community help room to feel like relief. Every outcome does not need the same “Live with it.” voice.

    Next playthrough

    From a natural opening: complete one leg, encounter consequential weather, affect a named crew member, rest, reach a town and resupply. Reload at a pending choice and after a consequence. Ask: Where are we? Who was affected? What changed? Why? What are we watching for next?
