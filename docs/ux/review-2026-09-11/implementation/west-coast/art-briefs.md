# Western art briefs and source files

Generated with the built-in Image Generation tool. The original prairie road and established journey atlas supplied the visual reference. These are retained generation specifications and source links, not a verbatim transcript of the tool prompts.

Road brief: 1536 × 1024, matching the game's illustrated 16-bit pixel style, palette and edge treatment. Preserve a side-on horizontal road at the same vertical position as the established road scene (roughly y=675–835, centerline near y=747), with an open van lane, layered regional landscape and foreground. No vehicles or people in the background; the normal van and its six seats are rendered independently. No embedded words, labels, UI or photorealistic textures. Runtime text remains translatable.

Atlas brief: 1536 × 1024, exactly two columns and three equal rows. Regional, crew-free town and camp settings in the established style; empty signs and foreground room for independent player/NPC portraits. Cells are presented through their matching viewBox rather than stretched into unrelated slots.
