# On the road

Travel carries your crew toward D.C. {{#help travel}} Use **Pause travel** when you want to inspect the situation before continuing.

![A stretch of the Dystrail road](/play/static/img/journey/road-classic.png)

## Set today's approach

Open **Pace · News intake** before the day's travel begins. You can change these settings until that day starts; then they lock until the next day.

### Travel pace

Choose **Steady**, **Heated**, or **Blitz**. {{#help pace}} A faster pace also raises encounter risk. It never guarantees a particular distance.

### Information diet

Choose **Quiet**, **Mixed**, or **Doomscroll**. {{#help news}}

### Weather and road conditions

{{#help weather}} A new day's forecast can differ from the one you saw before resting. Check the current conditions and your protection before choosing a long drive.

The option cards show the numbers for your current crew and conditions. After an action, its receipt reports the actual resource changes.

## Respond to interruptions

- **Encounters:** Read the situation and the known effects on each choice. After choosing, the next screen reports the actual resource changes.
- **Camp:** Opening camp costs nothing. {{#help camp}} Rest has a cooldown, so you cannot rely on repeating it immediately.
- **Towns:** {{#help town}} Stores sell supplies and equipment. {{#help trade}} The route map shows upcoming stops and services.
- **Crossings:** The available options and their costs depend on the crossing. A detour, permit, or payment may solve a checkpoint differently; read the current option card before committing.
- **Breakdowns:** Travel pauses until you address the van. {{#help repair}} A matching spare, an improvised fix, and roadside service have different costs.

### Finding more supplies

{{#help gather}}

The **Status & inventory** view shows your crew, equipment, vehicle, and supplies. The **Trail journal** records what happened. The route map traces your progress and shows what is ahead.
