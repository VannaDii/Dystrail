# On the road

Travel carries your crew toward D.C. The road can continue through ordinary stretches, then stop for a decision, a town, a crossing, a breakdown, or another event. Use **Pause travel** when you want to inspect the situation before continuing.

![A stretch of the Dystrail road](/play/static/img/journey/road-classic.png)

## Set today's approach

Open **Pace · News intake** before the day's travel begins. You can change these settings until that day starts; then they lock until the next day.

| Setting | Choices | What to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel pace | Steady, Heated, Blitz | A faster pace raises the speed limit but costs sanity as driving time accumulates and increases encounter risk. Weather, the van, and available driving time still affect actual distance. |
| Information diet | Quiet, Mixed, Doomscroll | Quiet supports sanity but lowers the extra receipt chance; Doomscroll raises that chance at a sanity cost. Its effect applies once per day. |

The option cards show the current numbers. Weather can change at the start of a day, so check the forecast and the action receipt instead of assuming that a pace guarantees a particular distance.

## Respond to interruptions

- **Encounters:** Read the situation and the known effects on each choice. After choosing, the next screen reports the actual resource changes.
- **Camp:** Opening camp costs nothing. Rest and other recovery actions show their time and resource costs. Rest has a cooldown, and a stationary day can still bring weather or hunger effects.
- **Towns:** Talk to locals, visit the store, or use an available community exchange before leaving. The route map shows upcoming stops and services.
- **Crossings:** The available options and their costs depend on the crossing. A detour, permit, or payment may solve a checkpoint differently; read the current option card before committing.
- **Breakdowns:** Travel pauses until you address the van. A matching spare, an improvised fix, or service can have different costs. The repair screen gives the current choices.

The **Status & inventory** view shows your crew, equipment, vehicle, and supplies. The **Trail journal** records what happened. The route map traces your progress and shows what is ahead.
