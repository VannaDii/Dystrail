# Start a journey

Open [Dystrail](https://dystrail.com/play/) and wait for the game to finish preparing its files. The setup screen then lets you choose a trail and use the generated run code or enter another valid one.

## Choose your trail

- **Classic:** {{#help classic}}
- **The Deep End:** {{#help deep}}

{{#help code}} Save a code if you want to replay its starting conditions in the same game version. A code alone does not preserve your progress. For an exact in-progress return, use a [save or backup](saving.md).

## Choose and name your crew

Pick one of six characters: Journalist, Organizer, Whistleblower, Lobbyist, Staffer, or Satirist. Their starting resources and advantages differ; the character screen previews them. Then enter your name and crew name. You can keep the suggested names for your companions or replace them.

## Outfit the van

{{#help outfitting}}

{{#help capacity}} Check the cart total and remaining cash before departure; removing an item returns its cost to your budget.

### What the equipment does

- **Food and water:** {{#help food_and_water}}
- **Spare parts:** {{#help spares}}
- **Protective gear:** {{#help protection}}
- **Documents:** {{#help documents}}

The departure review lists the selected loadout and what you will have left. Return to outfitting if the balance or supplies are wrong. Choose **Start the journey** to pay and depart.
