Dystopian Trail. Dystrail, for short.

This is the official home of Dystopian Trail, a free, single-player survival game inspired by The Oregon Trail. Its political satire takes aim at Trump-era tariffs, DOGE accounting, MAHA health claims, and government propaganda, with real-world source notes inside the game.

Choose from six personas, manage scarce supplies, make camp, and face encounters in Classic or The Deep End. Your choices shape who makes it to the hearing in D.C. Read the game guide or explore the open-source project.
